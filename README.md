# WhisperNotes-AI-Powered-Lecture-Intelligence-for-Students
This repository documents the end-to-end product design, customer research, and pricing strategy for WhisperNotes, an AI-powered lecture note-taking and learning assistant developed as part of MGMT 682: Digital Product Design.

WhisperNotes is designed for university students in lecture-heavy courses and transforms raw lectures into exam-ready learning assets—structured summaries, flashcards, and personalized quizzes—going beyond simple transcription tools.

🚀 Key Highlights

- Product Focus: AI-powered academic note-taking & learning assistant
- Core Methods: Survey research, Choice-Based Conjoint (CBC) analysis, Economic Value to Customer (EVC)
- Outcome: Data-backed product design, pricing, and go-to-market recommendation

🧠 The Product Vision

WhisperNotes records lectures, synchronizes spoken content with uploaded slides, and applies academic intelligence to identify:
- Key concepts and definitions
- xam cues and examples
- Structured summaries aligned with course objectives
- Unlike generic transcription tools, WhisperNotes focuses on learning outcomes, reducing cognitive load during lectures while improving exam preparation efficiency.

📊 Research & Analysis

To validate customer demand and pricing, we conducted a student survey (n = 41) followed by a Choice-Based Conjoint analysis.

1. Customer Discovery
- ~78% of respondents were likely or very likely to try an AI-powered note-taking tool
- Most students still rely on manual or fragmented note-taking methods
- Clear unmet need in organizing lecture content for exam prep

2. Conjoint Analysis (CBC)
We evaluated trade-offs across four attributes:
- Summary Quality
- Slide Synchronization
- Sample Quiz Generation
- Price (Free → $14.99)

Key insights:
- Summary Quality was the most important non-price attribute
- Incremental differentiation value of the full feature bundle: ~$8–$9/month
- Students value learning enhancement more than convenience features

3. Economic Value to the Customer (EVC)
Using time-savings and avoided app costs:
- Estimated EVC ≈ $187 per student per month
- Strong justification for value-based pricing with significant consumer surplus

💡 Strategic Recommendation
- Freemium model to drive adoption and data collection
- Pro plan at ~$4.99/month, capturing a portion of the $8–$9 revealed differentiation value
- Product roadmap prioritizes:
1 Summary quality
2 Quiz generation
3 Slide synchronization

Defined success metrics using a goal–driver–guardrail framework, emphasizing retention, engagement, and trust.
